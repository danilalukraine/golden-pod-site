About

Why we built a deterministic auditor.

No origin story. Just two things: why it runs on arithmetic first — and exactly what happens to your file.

01 — The manifest

Arithmetic first, AI second.

A finding is a work item — invoice, date, amount — something your team can actually close. Not a guess.

Rules detect it. Arithmetic matches it. A published formula scores it. AI comes next — but only on top of data this clean. Grounded first. Never invented.

A tool that decides silently isn't an auditor — it's a liability.
02 — Data policy

What happens to your file.

01
Analyzed in isolation
Each upload is processed on its own — never pooled, cross-referenced, or used to tune anything.
02
Deleted the moment the report exists
The original and every working copy are cleaned up in the same request — not a nightly job you have to trust.
03
Anything left over, purged in 48h
A background sweep clears temp files from failed requests — even the crash path has a deadline.
04
We keep the findings, only the findings
The report bundle is archived so your link keeps working. The original file is never part of it.
05
Links are unguessable, not logged-in
No accounts. A report opens only through its 64-bit token — "anyone with the link", with rate limiting on top.
